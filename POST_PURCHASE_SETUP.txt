LOCALREELS PRO — POST-PURCHASE ACCESS SETUP

1) Upload/deploy the entire LocalReelsPro_Sales_Site folder to Vercel.

2) In Vercel > Project > Settings > Environment Variables, add:
   STRIPE_SECRET_KEY = your CURRENT/ROTATED Stripe live secret key
   Do not put this value in index.html or any public file.

Optional stronger check:
   STRIPE_PAYMENT_LINK_ID = the Stripe Payment Link ID (starts with plink_) for the £47 LocalReels payment link.
   If supplied, the verification function will require the Checkout Session to come from that exact Payment Link.

3) Redeploy after adding/changing environment variables.

4) In Stripe Dashboard, open the £47 Payment Link, then its After Payment/confirmation settings. Set it to redirect customers to:
   https://localreelspro.co.uk/?access=1&session_id={CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}

5) Make one low-risk test purchase/test-mode test before launch. Confirm:
   - Stripe redirects to https://localreelspro.co.uk/?access=1&session_id=cs_...
   - The access page says Payment verified.
   - The complete ZIP downloads.
   - The 200-Prompt Vault and Legal & Compliance Bonus open.
   - A direct visit to /?access=1 without session_id does not reveal product links.
